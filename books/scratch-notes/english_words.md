:::details dilemma

ジレンマ

| part of speech | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| noun | a situation in which a difficult choice has to be made between two or more alternatives, often ones that are equally undesirable |

di-（2つの）+ lemma（前提・命題）。ギリシャ語由来。

:::

:::details nonetheless

それにもかかわらず

| part of speech | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| adverb | in spite of what has just been said |

nevertheless と同義。

:::

:::details rollout

（新製品・サービス・システムの）公開、展開

| part of speech | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| noun | the official introduction of a new product, service, or system, often done in stages |
| verb | to officially introduce, launch, or make a new product, service, or system available |

動詞のときはふつう `roll out` と二語で書く。

:::

:::

不一致、食い違い

| part of speech | meaning |

:::details audit

監査

| part of speech | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| noun | an official examination of accounts, records, or a process, and the report of that examination |
| verb | to make an official examination of the accounts of a business and produce a report |

inspection が対象そのものを見て確かめるのに対し、audit は記録や手順を基準に照らして体系的に調べる。

[inspection との違い](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/omarrahmoun_audit-and-inspection-are-both-quality-control-activity-7326968958354382848-d1wr)

:::

:::details quantitative

定量的な、量的な

| part of speech | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| adjective | relating to quantity; measured or expressed in numbers rather than by quality |

対義語は qualitative（定性的な）。

> The skill generation benchmarks provide a quantitative evaluation of skill quality, which is increasingly critical as manual quality assessment becomes challenging nowadays.

:::

:::details in place

準備が整っている、所定の位置にある

| part of speech | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| phrase | established and ready to be used; in the correct position |

> I wanted tests in place before refactoring this plugin, but I had never written tests for a Neovim plugin.
>
> このプラグインをリファクタリングする前に、テストが整備されている（準備できている）状態にしたかった。

:::

:::details shada

Neovim の共有データファイル（ShaDa = SHAred DAta）

| part of speech | meaning |
| --- | --- |
| noun | Neovim's file for session state such as command history, search history, marks, and registers. It corresponds to Vim's viminfo |

> Environment variables are inherited by the child processes, so every Neovim in the run sees the same isolated directories, and swap files and shada written during tests no longer land in the user's state directory.

:::
